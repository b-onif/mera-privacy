mera Privacy Policy

Effective 23 November 2026

mera is a local-first pole dance training and choreography app developed and operated by Victoria Onifade. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Information stored on your device

Your movements, practice plans and history, choreography, settings, notes, and imported videos are stored locally on your device. Imported videos are copied into mera-managed app storage. mera does not require an account and does not upload this content to a mera-operated server.

Photo library access

When you choose to import a video, mera accesses the selected item from your photo library. It may use the photo-library asset identifier and recording date to detect duplicate imports and preserve the video’s original recording date. mera does not scan or upload your photo library. Deleting an imported video from mera removes the app’s stored copy and does not delete the original video from your Photos library.

Music link metadata

When you enter a recognised Spotify, Apple Music, or YouTube link for a choreography and the song title is empty, mera automatically contacts that provider to retrieve the page title. The provider receives the request under its own privacy policy and may receive standard connection information such as your IP address. The link and retrieved title remain in mera’s local app data; mera does not send them to a mera-operated server.

Data collection and tracking

mera does not include advertising, third-party analytics, or cross-app tracking. The developer does not collect your in-app content through the app.

Retention, backups, and deletion

Local records and imported videos remain on your device until you delete them or remove the app. mera does not currently provide its own backup service. Because mera stores its data and managed video copies in the app’s Application Support directory, they may be included in device or iCloud backups according to your Apple settings. Deleting mera removes its locally stored app data from that device, subject to any backups managed by Apple.

Your choices and privacy rights

You can review and delete content within mera, or remove the app to delete its locally stored data from that device. You can control photo-library permission in your device’s Settings. For privacy questions, requests, or concerns about your applicable data-protection rights, contact the developer using the email address below. You may also have the right to complain to your local data-protection authority, including the UK Information Commissioner’s Office.

Contact

mera is developed and operated by Victoria Onifade. For privacy questions or requests, email Bukky.onifade@outlook.com.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when mera’s features or data practices change. The effective date above will be updated when changes are published.
