mera Privacy Policy

Effective 23 November 2026

mera is a local-first pole dance training and choreography app. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Information stored on your device

Your movements, practice plans and history, choreography, settings, notes, and imported videos are stored locally on your device. Imported videos are copied into mera-managed app storage. mera does not require an account and does not upload this content to a mera-operated server.

Photo library access

When you choose to import a video, mera accesses the selected item from your photo library. It may use the photo-library asset identifier and recording date to detect duplicate imports and preserve the video’s original recording date. mera does not scan or upload your photo library.

Music link metadata

If you enter a recognised Spotify, Apple Music, or YouTube link for a choreography, mera may contact that provider to retrieve the page title. The provider receives the request under its own privacy policy and may receive standard connection information such as your IP address. The link and retrieved title remain in mera’s local app data; mera does not send them to a mera-operated server.

Data collection and tracking

mera does not include advertising, third-party analytics, or cross-app tracking. The developer does not collect your in-app content through the app.

Backups and deletion

mera does not currently provide its own backup service. Data may be included in device backups according to your Apple device and iCloud settings. You can delete individual content in the app. Deleting mera removes its locally stored app data from that device, subject to any backups managed by Apple.

Contact

For privacy questions, email Bukky.onifade@outlook.com.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when mera’s features or data practices change. The effective date above will be updated when changes are published.
